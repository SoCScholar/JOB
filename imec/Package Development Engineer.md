## About imec Germany

The automotive industry is undergoing a major transformation, with advanced chiplet technology paving the way for autonomous driving and connected vehicles.

Imec and the State of Baden-Württemberg are establishing a joint project to support the local and international automotive industry in derisking and accelerating the introduction of automotive chiplets into manufacturing.

At the new **Advanced Chip Design Accelerator (ACDA)** competence center, based at Innovation Park AI (IPAI) in Heilbronn, imec aims to push the boundaries of semiconductor innovation and accelerate the future of automotive technology.

By leveraging imec's world-leading expertise in semiconductor technology and packaging, the center will develop chiplet-based solutions that enable future vehicles to become smarter, safer, and more efficient.

## Role: Package Development Engineer

### Responsibilities

#### 1. Package Selection and Analysis

- Select appropriate IC package types based on given specifications and IC requirements.
- Perform comprehensive analysis of the electrical characteristics of selected packages.
- Identify limitations and potential issues during the design process.

#### 2. Electrical Performance Optimization

- Optimize the electrical performance of IC packages, focusing on:
  - Signal Integrity (SI).
  - Power Integrity (PI).
  - Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC).
- Analyze routing, parasitic effects, and noise issues associated with package designs.
- Propose design modifications and enhancements to improve electrical performance.

#### 3. Design for Manufacturability and Cost Optimization

- Evaluate IC package designs for manufacturability and cost efficiency.
- Review compatibility with:
  - Manufacturing processes.
  - Assembly requirements.
  - Material availability.
- Recommend design modifications to streamline manufacturing while maintaining or reducing overall costs.

#### 4. Documentation and Reporting

- Prepare comprehensive technical reports covering:
  - Package analysis.
  - Design modifications.
  - Optimization recommendations.
  - Simulation results.
  - Engineering calculations.
  - Experimental results.
- Include detailed diagrams and supporting technical documentation.
- Communicate the rationale behind design decisions and their potential impact on IC performance and reliability.
